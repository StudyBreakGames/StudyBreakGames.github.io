About StudyBreakGames

```

Welcome to StudyBreakGames — a place for quick, simple and entertaining browser games you can play whenever you need a break.

Our goal is to make it easy to discover games that are fun, accessible and don't require complicated downloads or installations.

Whether you have five minutes between tasks or just want something fun to play, StudyBreakGames is built to give you an easy place to take a break.

Why StudyBreakGames?

We believe games should be easy to find and easy to play. That's why we're building a growing collection of browser-based games that you can access directly from the site.

We're continuously working on improving the site and adding more games for visitors to enjoy.
